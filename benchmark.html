Assessment integrity

Personality and capability assessments on this site are designed as structured self-assessment and development instruments. They are not clinical diagnoses and should not be presented as validated hiring tests until reliability, construct validity, criterion validity, fairness and local legal requirements have been established for the intended use.

For employment decisions, use multiple job-related evidence sources and a structured process. OPM notes that structured interviews, work samples and other methods differ in validity, cost and subgroup impact; no single score should be treated as a complete representation of a person.
