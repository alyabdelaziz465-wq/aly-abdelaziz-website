تحليلات الأفراد

From an HR metric to a decision.

A metric starts a chain: what happened, why, what will we do, and did it work?

Measure

Turnover, time-to-hire, absence, engagement, performance and skill gaps.

Diagnose

Find patterns, bottlenecks and factors associated with the outcome.

Intervene

Choose an action, owner and expected result.

Evaluate

Compare the result with the baseline and assess impact.

Decision Intelligence

Use analytics to improve decisions, not just dashboards.

Privacy & Ethics

Use appropriate data, control access and explain how it informs decisions.

مستقبل العمل

The future is not just adopting AI — it is redesigning work.

Ask what should be automated, augmented or human-led, and what skills and governance are required.

Automation

Automate repeatable work when value is clear and risk is controllable.

Augmentation

Use AI to increase human speed, analysis, creativity and decision support.

Human-led

Keep accountability, empathy, ethics and high-impact people decisions human-led.

Skills-based Organization

Move beyond job titles toward skills, capability and mobility.

AI Agents

Use workflow agents with clear permissions, auditability and escalation.

AI-ready Leadership

Lead adoption through governance, communication, reskilling and measurable outcomes.
