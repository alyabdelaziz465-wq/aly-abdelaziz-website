- World Economic Forum Future of Jobs 2025

## Content architecture
Every deep article follows: Concept → Theory → Diagnosis → Implementation → Tools → Measurement → Red Team → Aly Perspective.

## Important assessment note
The assessments are structured self-assessment/development instruments. They are not clinical diagnoses and are not presented as validated employment-selection tests. Production use for selection requires a proper validation program, job analysis, fairness review, legal review and ongoing monitoring.
