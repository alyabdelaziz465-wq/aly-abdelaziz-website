EXPERT PROFILE

Aly Abdelaziz

HR Consultant specializing in Strategic HR, KPIs, Balanced Scorecard, Performance Management, Talent, Leadership, People Analytics and AI in HR.

Aly’s work is built around a simple question: how can HR improve the quality of business and people decisions? The platform connects strategy, evidence, diagnosis, human judgment, technology and measurement into one practical architecture.

Strategic HR & Workforce Planning

Employee Engagement & Experience
People Analytics & AI in HR

How Aly approaches problems

Business → Evidence → Diagnosis → Decision → Action → Impact. This means resisting one-size-fits-all HR answers, making assumptions explicit and using measurement to learn.
