AI MOCK INTERVIEW

Practice the follow-up, not just the question

Prototype flow: the next question should adapt to your answer — “What did you do?”, “What was the result?”, “How did you measure it?”, “What would you do differently?”

Stage 1 — Tell me about yourself

Answer in writing for the MVP. Voice input can be added as the next technical layer.

Follow-up: What was your specific contribution, and what evidence shows the result?

What the full version will analyse

Structure, relevance, evidence, clarity, value proposition, role fit, follow-up resilience and self-awareness.
