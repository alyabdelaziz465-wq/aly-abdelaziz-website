AI & THE FUTURE OF WORK

AI should improve the work — not replace professional judgment.

The practical question is not “Where can we use AI?” It is “Where does AI create value, what risks does it introduce, and where must a human remain accountable?”

01Automate

Repeatable, rules-based work where quality and risk can be controlled.

02Augment

Research, analysis, drafting and decision support with human review.

03Human-led

High-impact people decisions where context, fairness and accountability matter.

AI Across the Employee Lifecycle

Recruitment, learning, workforce planning, performance, mobility and employee support — with clear human oversight.

Skills-Based Work

Move beyond job titles toward skills, capabilities, internal mobility and continuous reskilling.

Responsible AI

Purpose, data quality, privacy, bias, permissions, explainability, auditability and human escalation.

INTERACTIVE ADVISORS

Useful tools for real career and hiring decisions.

These are not score calculators. They guide the user through evidence, questions and practical recommendations. They do not invent qualifications or make final hiring decisions.

01

CV & Target Role Advisor

Upload a TXT or PDF CV, add the target role and job description, then review the CV from an applicant-tracking, recruiter-readability and evidence perspective.

TXT and PDF are supported. PDF text extraction runs in the browser.
The advisor will not invent employers, dates, qualifications or achievements. Always review changes before using them.
02

Professional Assessment Advisor

Explore professional judgment, career direction, leadership or talent-acquisition capability through job-relevant scenarios and reflective questions.

This is a developmental assessment, not a clinical personality test and not a standalone hiring decision.
